# Route explainability V1

Every returned stop carries structured codes, not generated prose. Inclusion
codes identify strict eligibility and Stage 2 utility, plus a real popularity or
exposure bonus when applicable. Ordering codes identify the route objective,
actual configured transitions, meal-window alignment, rest after high activity,
and final overnight accommodation. Each stop also preserves the Tourism
Service2 source, allowed/conditional pet verdict, trust level, full place
utility, dwell source, timestamps, and schedule warnings.

Up to five omitted high-utility places receive one bounded reason:
`time_window_conflict`, `excessive_detour`, `category_redundancy`,
`lower_total_route_score`, `unresolved_travel_edge`, `pace_limit`,
`accommodation_position_rule`, or `final_validation_failure`. These are
calculation labels only; they do not speculate about provider facts.

Alternatives come only from validated finalists. Lower travel requires at
least 90% of the main objective; popularity-oriented requires at least 85% and
a sufficient-data popular anchor. At most two are returned. No alternative can
weaken eligibility, use unresolved travel, or omit exact final validation.
